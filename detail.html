A closer look at my Canada.ca website redesign research for CCT380.

Canada.ca Website Redesign

For CCT380, I explored how Canada.ca could become easier to navigate for international students who speak English as a second language.

The Challenge

Government acronyms, formal language, and department-based navigation can make important services harder to find.

My Research

I worked with three international ESL students at UTM. They completed a short survey and a navigation task about finding Employment Insurance eligibility information.

Proposed Improvements

My redesign direction focused on clearer labels, navigation organized around users' goals, fewer nested menus, and more visible actions.

Reflection

This project strengthened my interest in user research and information architecture.
